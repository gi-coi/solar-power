# EU funding for public solar tenders — instruments, mechanisms, beneficiaries

Research note compiled 2026-11-28 by a funding/beneficiary sub-agent. Scope: which EU
money finances solar procurement by public bodies, how it reaches TED-published tenders,
and who the main beneficiaries are. Complements `public-tender-solar-research.md` (policy
demand side) and the TED dataset (~5,000 EU solar award notices).

Conventions: **(A)** = established against a primary source (Commission/EUR-Lex/ECA);
**(B)** = reported/derived, not fully verified, or contested. Figures with ⚠️ are
unverified against a primary source and listed in `uncertainties.md`.

---

## 1. Funding instruments

### (A) Recovery & Resilience Facility / REPowerEU — the dominant stream
- **RRF total: €577bn committed** (after the end-2025 amendments), of which **€400bn
  disbursed** by May 2026. **Energy-related measures ≈ €153.8bn (27% of the fund)**;
  climate expenditure ≈ €235bn; green-transition share averages 41% (min. 37%).
  [Commission DG ENER, "RRF for clean energy"](#84)
- **Renewables ≈ €26.4bn** allocated to "faster deployment of renewable energy"; **energy
  efficiency in buildings ≈ €75.4bn** (residential + public + social housing); **grids,
  storage, smart meters ≈ €24.6bn**. The building-efficiency line is the largest channel
  through which *public bodies* (schools, hospitals, offices) fund rooftop solar as part
  of renovation packages. [DG ENER](#84)
- **REPowerEU chapters**: the RRF is "at the heart" of REPowerEU; the chapters total
  **€54.3bn committed** against the ~€300bn the Commission said the plan needed, split
  roughly €35bn (efficiency/renewables/industrial decarbonisation) + €17bn
  (grid/interconnection/storage/mobility) + €6bn (demand reduction). REPowerEU grants of
  ~€20bn were allocated to member states. [ECA SR 21/2026](#85); [Commission REPowerEU](#100)

### (A) Cohesion Policy 2021–2027 (ERDF + Cohesion Fund + JTF)
- **€43.7bn** across all cohesion funds is allocated to **energy priorities** — the
  second-largest EU energy source after the RRF. Total Cohesion Policy is ~€392bn
  (initial allocation); ERDF+CF envelope ≈ €367bn. [DG ENER](#88)
- **(B) Renewables-specific share is much smaller**: an academic comparison puts the
  ERDF/CF renewables capacity envelope at **~€8bn** (vs ~€26.4bn RRF renewables), and
  notes member-state portfolios skew to wind (Poland's RRF portfolio 92.9% wind) or
  "other". ⚠️ solar-specific share not isolated. [Nigohosyan, Economies 2026](#103)

### (A) Modernisation Fund (ETS-funded, 13 lower-income MS)
- **€23.2bn disbursed** across **345 investment proposals** so far (Bulgaria, Croatia,
  Czechia, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Portugal, Romania,
  Slovakia, Slovenia). **Poland holds the largest allowance share (43.41%** in the first
  category; ~172m allowances); Romania, Czechia next. [MF portal](#90); [Commission](#89)
- **Solar features prominently** in national calls. Romania's MF solar programmes include
  ~€415m + €400m + €310.7m (self-consumption/generation) and **€500m for solar + storage
  at public entities**; the MF also backs Romania's Contracts-for-Difference (1.53 GW Dec
  2024 + 2.75 GW Aug 2025). [Momentum Energy / MF guide](#92)
- A 2026 round disbursed **€2.5bn** to 51 projects in 11 MS (Czechia €516.8m, Romania
  €636.9m, Hungary €552.3m, Greece €233.9m, Poland €180m, …). [MF press release](#91)

### (A) Innovation Fund (ETS, managed by CINEA)
- **~€40bn expected revenue 2020–2030**; funds net-zero *manufacturing* and *deployment*
  via calls/auctions. **(B)** Recent solar awards: **MOD4PV** (Spain, 1.5 GW heterojunction
  module assembly), **SOLAR4EU** (France, PV manufacturing), **WATTRAXIS** (France, floating
  PV). 2023 call: €4.8bn to 85 projects; 2025 call: €3.4bn to 77 projects; ~€7.1bn
  committed by end-2024. [Innovation Fund projects](#93); [Renewables Now](#94); [AKSR](#106)
- Note: Innovation Fund money is mostly **grants to companies**, not classic public
  procurement — it is a *manufacturer/developer* channel, distinct from TED tenders.

### (A) Smaller / supporting instruments
- **Just Transition Fund**: ~€19.3bn (€19.7bn incl. transfers); JTM mobilises ~€55bn for
  coal/lignite regions; renewables one eligible use. [Commission JTF](#96)
- **InvestEU**: €26.2bn EU budget guarantee; **€397bn mobilised by end-2025** (loans/
  guarantees, incl. clean-energy); a "Just Transition" scheme of €10–15bn. [DG ENER](#88); [EPRS](#105)
- **LIFE – Clean Energy Transition sub-programme**: ~€1bn (2021–2027); >€450m for 260+
  CET projects since 2021 (capacity-building, not hardware). [DG ENER](#88)
- **CEF Energy / EU renewable-energy financing mechanism (RENEWFM)**: CEF Energy €5.8bn;
  RENEWFM cross-border tenders: 1st €27.5m (7 solar PV, Finland, 283 MW), 2nd €46m
  (Finland/Estonia, 386 MW), 3rd call €54.9m (ground-mounted PV, Bulgaria/Finland). CEF
  CB-RES window allocated ~€126m to 3 cross-border renewables projects. [RENEWFM](#97); [eubusiness](#98)

### (A) National co-financing
All EU instruments co-finance: cohesion funds require national co-financing (EU share
50–85% by region); RRF grants are paid against milestones but projects are implemented
with national budget top-ups. Net effect: headline EU figures understate total public
spend on solar tenders; no EU-wide ratio is published. ⚠️

## 2. How the money reaches public tenders

- **(A) RRF/REPowerEU**: disbursed *via national Recovery & Resilience Plans* (all 27
  revised RRPs include a REPowerEU chapter; >110 amendments since 2021). Money is paid to
  national treasuries against milestones/targets, then spent by line ministries and, for
  public-building solar, by **municipalities, schools, hospitals and public housing
  operators** — often through **public procurement published on TED/national portals**.
  [DG ENER](#84)
- **(A) Cohesion Policy**: managed by **Managing Authorities** in **operational
  programmes** (national/regional). Beneficiary public bodies contract via public
  procurement; projects appear in **Kohesio** (1.5m projects, ~500k beneficiaries).
  [Kohesio](#101)
- **(A) Modernisation Fund**: proposed by member states, assessed by the EIB, disbursed
  on Commission decisions; money flows to **public bodies and companies via national
  calls** (e.g. Romania's "public entities" solar+storage call) and CfD auctions.
  [MF guide](#92)
- **(B) Grant vs. tender split is not quantified EU-wide.** A large share of RRF solar
  flows as **grants/subsidies to households and firms** (prosumer voucher schemes, e.g.
  Romania's household PV voucher, Italy's rooftop incentives), which do **not** appear on
  TED; only the public-body share does. ⚠️ No primary source isolates the TED-published
  fraction. [DG ENER](#84); [DW/EDJ](#104)
- **(A) Direct Commission/CINEA tenders** (RENEWFM, Innovation Fund, CEF) are
  competitive calls/grants run centrally, not classic TED public procurement — a distinct
  channel from the municipal tenders in our dataset.

## 3. Main beneficiaries

### Member states (largest EU funding for solar/renewables)
- **(A)** By **REPowerEU chapter allocation** (ECA Annex II, Oct 2025): **Poland €24.5bn
  (37.8%)**, Italy €11.2bn (17.1%), Spain €6.9bn (10.6%), Hungary €4.6bn (7.1%), Croatia
  €2.9bn, France €2.8bn, Germany €2.4bn, Romania €2.0bn. [ECA SR 21/2026](#86)
- **(A)** By **RRF renewables allocation**: **Italy, Spain, Poland** largest in absolute
  terms (Luxembourg's entire RRF energy share is renewables). [DG ENER](#84)
- **(A)** Modernisation Fund: **Poland** largest share (43.4%); recent rounds favour
  Czechia/Romania/Hungary. [Commission MF](#89); [MF round](#91)
- This matches our TED dataset's country skew (Poland, Czechia, Spain) — Poland's large
  RRF/MF allocation plausibly drives its ~40% share of solar award notices.

### Public bodies / sectors
- **(B)** Main buyers are **municipalities** (rooftops on municipal buildings, schools,
  swimming pools, depots), **schools**, **hospitals**, and **social/public-housing
  operators** — typically via framework agreements, DPS, or central purchasing bodies
  (LASER/Kent, Portsmouth), and increasingly **energy communities**. ⚠️ no EU-wide
  breakdown of *which* body types buy most solar. [DW/EDJ](#104); earlier notes #60–#64
- France's RRF explicitly funds renovation of "state-owned public buildings" and "social
  housing"; the EPBD Art. 10 standard (existing public buildings by 2027/28/30) is the
  structural demand driver. [DG ENER](#84); sources #82

### Companies / contractors
- **(A)** The clearest named case is the **FSR Romania** probe: **LONGi Solar Technologie
  GmbH** (consortium with **ENEVO**) and **Shanghai Electric** (UK/HK subsidiaries, Chinese
  SOE) bid for the Rovinari 454.97 MW PV park (~€375m, **partly EU-financed**) and
  **withdrew** after the Commission opened an in-depth FSR investigation (April 2024).
  [Breton statement](#99); earlier notes #42–#44
- **(B)** Large EPCs/developers active across Europe: **Ameresco + Sunel** (1.5 GWp
  pipeline), **GES** (US$252m, 4 Spanish PV EPCs), **TBEA SunOasis/Sungrow/Huawei**
  (inverters, 55% global market). ⚠️ no TED-derived ranking of who *wins* EU-funded solar
  tenders exists — the missing common supplier ID (ECA) blocks it. [pv-tech/Wiki-Solar via search](#103)
- **(A)** The **China/NZIA-FSR interaction** is the live controversy: NZIA Art. 25
  resilience criteria (PV >85% single-country trigger) + FSR (≥€250m notification) aim to
  tilt EU-funded procurement away from Chinese bidders; Italy's FER-X excludes Chinese
  cells/modules/inverters; the two FSR solar probes ended in withdrawal (deterrent, no
  remedy). [public-tender-solar-research.md §4]

## 4. Data sources & studies

- **(A) Authoritative trackers**: **RRF Recovery & Resilience Scoreboard** (thematic
  "Clean power"/green analyses); **Kohesio** (cohesion projects + beneficiaries);
  **Cohesion Open Data Platform** (150 programmes, >€110bn); **TED**; **Modernisation
  Fund portal** (per-project disbursements); **Innovation Fund dashboard**. [DG ENER](#84); [Kohesio](#101)
- **(A) Key audit — ECA SR 21/2026** ("REPowerEU needs a boost", 9 Sept 2026): only
  **€54.3bn of ~€300bn** committed; **renewables far short of the 103 GW target**; RRF
  chapters make "only a limited contribution" to clean generation; **"EU funding has
  played only a minor role in ending reliance on Russian oil and gas"**; governance/
  monitoring gaps; Poland 38% concentration. [ECA](#85); [EU Insider](#87)
- **(A) Earlier audits** already logged: **ECA SR 28/2023** (procurement data gaps,
  lowest-price dominance), **ECA SR 09/2025** (RRF procurement/state-aid compliance "not
  assured") — directly relevant to "who benefits" transparency. [sources #53, #54]
- **(B) Academic/think-tank**: Nigohosyan (Economies 2026) — CPF ~€8bn renewables vs RRF
  ~€26.4bn; wind-skewed portfolios; ⚠️ figures drawn from Scoreboard/Cohesion platform.
  [Nigohosyan](#103)
- **Key gap**: no authoritative **solar-specific** quantification of EU-funded public
  procurement or its beneficiaries exists — the same evidence gap our TED dataset targets.
